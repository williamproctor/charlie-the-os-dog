# Charlie — Video Short Storyboards (Pre-Production v1)

Eight boards, two lanes. Frames are gpt-image-2 keyframes (9:16, 864×1536) rendered against the
locked style references — they are **storyboard/prototype grade**: final keyframes get re-rendered
against the locked clay canon (see charlie-style-decision.html) before any clip ships.

**Global spec (applies to every board)**
- Duration: 8–15s total (revised from 15–30s — Veo native clip limit; stitching causes drift)
- Aspect: 9:16 vertical, native upload, burned-in captions where noted
- Audio: ambient room tone only + at most ONE audible sigh. No music (licensing + brand rule).
- Camera: locked-off at Charlie's eye level unless noted. No pet-cam angles.
- Voice: Charlie never speaks. Captions carry the setup; the reaction is the punchline.
- i2v: generate each shot from its approved keyframe (image-to-video), NOT text-to-video.
  Reactive lane → fast tiers (Veo Fast / Seedance fast). Evergreen lane → quality tiers.
- Disclosure: "Made with AI" toggle on X; AI note in first two sentences on LinkedIn.

**Lanes**
- SB-01…04 — Lane C launch-adjacent evergreens → claymation hero lane (craft signal)
- SB-05…08 — discourse-beat evergreens → clay, reactive slot (single locked lane per the style decision)

Per-board docs live in each folder: `storyboard.md` beside `frame-1..4.png`.
